About Amanda

Online or In-Person Hypnotherapy — Wherever You Feel Most Comfortable

My name is Amanda and I am a fully qualified Solution Focused Hypnotherapist, helping people move towards a calmer, more positive future.

You can choose to attend sessions online from the comfort of your own home, or visit my therapy room in Farsley, Leeds, West Yorkshire.

All you need for an online session is a quiet space, a phone, tablet or computer and a reliable internet connection.

My Approach

I provide a warm, supportive and non-judgemental environment where you can focus on making positive changes and moving towards the future you want.

Solution Focused Hypnotherapy combines modern neuroscience, psychotherapy and hypnosis techniques to help clients build resilience, confidence and emotional wellbeing.

Every client is different and every journey is unique. Sessions are tailored to your individual circumstances, goals and aspirations.

Whether you are struggling with anxiety, stress, poor sleep, low confidence or unwanted habits, my aim is to help you develop the tools and mindset needed to move forward positively.
