How Hypnotherapy Works

Relaxing, Positive and Focused on Your Future

Hypnotherapy is not the same as stage hypnotism. It is not about showmanship, mind control or making you do things against your will.

When used as a therapy, hypnosis is a powerful tool that can help create positive change in a calm, relaxing and supportive environment.

Throughout the process you remain fully aware, fully in control and able to hear everything that is being said.

The Science Behind Solution Focused Hypnotherapy

Solution Focused Hypnotherapy uses the latest research in neuroscience to help us understand how the brain works and why we think, feel and react in certain ways.

Rather than focusing heavily on the past, this approach concentrates on helping you move towards the future you want to create.

By understanding how stress, anxiety and negative thinking affect the brain, we can work together to encourage healthier thought patterns and improve emotional wellbeing.

What Is Trance?

Trance is a naturally occurring state of focused attention and deep relaxation that everyone experiences every day.

You may recognise it from moments when you become completely absorbed in a book, a film, music or even while driving a familiar route.

During trance the mind becomes calmer, distractions reduce and it is easier to focus on positive ideas, solutions and goals.

Most clients simply describe the experience as peaceful, comfortable and deeply relaxing.

How Solution Focused Hypnotherapy Helps

Solution Focused Hypnotherapy combines talking therapy with guided relaxation.

During our sessions we explore your goals, identify what is already working well and focus on practical steps that move you closer to the future you want.

While you are relaxed, positive suggestions can be introduced that help reinforce useful thoughts, behaviours and habits.

This allows your subconscious mind to explore solutions and develop new ways of responding to challenges, helping you build resilience, confidence and emotional wellbeing.

What Can It Help With?

Solution Focused Hypnotherapy can help with a wide range of issues, including stress, anxiety, low mood, confidence, fears, phobias, sleep difficulties and unwanted habits.

Every person is unique, which is why each programme is tailored to your individual circumstances and goals.
